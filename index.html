FAQ

Common questions

Which kinds of products do you name?

Pharmaceutical and biotech products, medical devices, diagnostics and clinical trials, as well as start-ups and corporate brands in healthcare.

How is AI used in your work?

AI helps us explore more territory and screen options faster. Senior specialists lead the strategy and make the creative and final recommendations.

Do you also design logos and identities?

Yes. Name and logo development can be scoped together, and we also work on corporate identities and rebranding.

Do you handle trademark clearance or regulatory approval?

Trademark clearance and regulatory submissions are handled by your legal and regulatory advisers. We develop names with those reviews in mind; the exact scope is agreed per project.

Where are your clients based?

Our clients are based in the European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and the USA.
